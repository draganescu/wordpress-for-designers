The V60 has a reputation for being fussy. It is not. It only asks you to do the same thing twice in a row.

Start with the recipe on the bag: 15 grams of coffee, 250 grams of water, three minutes. Use a scale. Guessing is the only real mistake you can make.

Wet the grounds with 50 grams of water and wait. This is the bloom: the coffee releases gas, and the water that follows can reach all of it evenly.

Change one thing at a time

If the cup is sour and thin, grind finer. If it is bitter and dry, grind coarser. Leave the water, the dose and the time alone until the grind is right.

Then, and only then, play. Hotter water for lighter roasts. A slower pour for more body. Write down what you did; you will not remember tomorrow.

A scale costs less than two bags of coffee and improves every bag after it.

Sam Okafor, tasting room
