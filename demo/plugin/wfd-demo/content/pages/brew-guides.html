Three recipes, tested on hundreds of sleepy mornings. Start here, then change one thing at a time.

15 g coffee · 250 g water · 3 minutes

V60

  1. Rinse the paper and warm the cup.
  2. Add 15 g of medium-fine coffee and 50 g of water just off the boil. Wait 40 seconds.
  3. Pour to 150 g in slow circles, then to 250 g.
  4. It should finish dripping at about three minutes.
16 g coffee · 220 g water · 2 minutes

AeroPress

  1. Set the AeroPress upside down and add 16 g of medium-ground coffee.
  2. Pour 220 g of water at 90 °C and stir three times.
  3. Fit the cap at one minute, flip at 1:30.
  4. Press gently for thirty seconds and stop at the hiss.
18 g in · 38 g out · 28 seconds

Espresso

  1. Dose 18 g and tamp level; pressure matters less than flatness.
  2. Aim for 38 g in the cup in 26 to 30 seconds.
  3. Too sour? Grind finer. Too bitter? Grind coarser.
  4. Taste it before you add milk. Always.
