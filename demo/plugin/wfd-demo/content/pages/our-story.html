Our story

One roaster and a bad idea.

In 2017 we bought a second-hand five-kilo roaster and put it in a garage that was too small for it. The idea was simple and, by most accounts, bad business: roast only what was ordered, and pay growers what the coffee was worth.

Eight years on the roaster is bigger and the garage is a tannery, but the idea has not moved. Nothing sits on a shelf. Every bag has a roast date and a farm on it, and every farm has a price you can look up.

We are fourteen people now. We still taste every batch before it leaves.

How we buy

We pay for the cup, not the commodity.

Every coffee we roast is bought directly, at a price agreed before the harvest. Last year that came to a little over twice the Fairtrade minimum.

  • Prices published with every lot
  • Three-year agreements, not one-off deals
  • One percent of revenue to the growers' co-ops
