Sharp, juicy and unmistakably Kenyan. Blackcurrant first, grapefruit after.

From the Gatomboya co-operative in Nyeri, on the slopes of Mount Kenya. SL28 and SL34, double washed and dried slowly on raised beds.

Brew it

  • V60: 15 g to 255 g, three minutes
  • Try it iced: brew over 90 g of ice
  • Best from day 10 to day 40 after roasting
