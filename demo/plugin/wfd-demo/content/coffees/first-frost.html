The winter blend. Spiced plum and cocoa, with orange peel at the finish.

Two lots from Huehuetenango, Guatemala: a washed Bourbon and a honey-processed Caturra, roasted separately and blended after. Here until February, or until it runs out.

Brew it

  • Cafetière: 30 g to 500 g, four minutes
  • Espresso: 18 g in, 40 g out
  • Very good with something baked
