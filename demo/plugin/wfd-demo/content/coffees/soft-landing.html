Decaf for people who love coffee. Milk chocolate and almond, with nothing missing but the caffeine.

Grown by the Triunfo Verde co-operative in Chiapas and decaffeinated with mountain water at Descamex in Veracruz. No solvents, at any stage.

Brew it

  • Any brewer; grind a touch finer than usual
  • Espresso: 18 g in, 36 g out, 30 seconds
  • Best within four weeks of roasting
