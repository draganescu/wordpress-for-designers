Sweet, round and happy in any brewer. The coffee we give to people who say they do not like fancy coffee.

From the Ortega family’s farm above the Magdalena river in Huila. Caturra and Colombia varieties, washed and dried under cover for three weeks.

Brew it

  • AeroPress: 16 g to 220 g, two minutes
  • Lovely as espresso at 18 g in, 40 g out
  • Best from day 5 to day 35 after roasting
