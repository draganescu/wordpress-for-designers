Our espresso blend. Dense, sweet and built to cut through milk without tasting of ash.

Sixty percent natural Brazil from Fazenda Santa Inês for body, forty percent washed Guatemala from Huehuetenango for sweetness. Roasted a little further than anything else we make.

Brew it

  • Espresso: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28 seconds
  • Rest it for ten days before you judge it
  • Makes a serious flat white
