The coffee we drink first. Floral, bright and very hard to stop pouring.

Grown by 240 smallholders around the Dambi Uddo washing station in Guji, at 2,100 metres. Washed, fermented for 48 hours and dried on raised beds for fifteen days.

Brew it

  • V60: 15 g to 250 g, three minutes
  • Water at 96 °C; it can take the heat
  • Best from day 7 to day 30 after roasting
